\section{Discussion and limitations}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What to report}
A method that changes the rotary base, the temperature and per-band gains at once should report a control that keeps its base and temperature and drops the rest; here that control ties \segaofficial{} on every reward model and on crop MUSIQ, and the remaining gap is \hoa{tsb}{musiq} full-resolution MUSIQ points. The global temperature is the cheapest of the three knobs. Claims that credit spectral or content adaptivity should be measured against this control with full-resolution metrics, because the 224-pixel scorers cannot see where such methods act.

\paragraph{Small dev sets mislead}
Eight dev prompts were enough to reject clearly worse variants and too few for anything else. On the dev set \tempsb{} looked slightly better than \segaofficial{} on MUSIQ, yet held out it is \hoa{tsb}{musiq} points worse; the one variant that passed the second gate lost on two metrics held out; and a rerun of \segaofficial{} on the same dev prompts moved MUSIQ by more than the margin of the one variant that passed the third gate (\cref{app:dev}). The held-out set has now been inspected and cannot serve as fresh confirmation for new variants.

\paragraph{Limitations}
We study one model family (\krea) at one square size, $4096^2$; other aspect ratios, sizes, backbones and video are untested, and \cref{sec:checkpoint} suggests results may not transfer. All metrics are automatic: the reward models see 224 pixels, MUSIQ and CLIP-IQA are learned proxies with their own biases, the spectral error uses a mean reference profile rather than a per-prompt target, and we ran no human study. Most arms have one seed; the seed-1 replication covers two arms against \segaofficial{} on \N{so@n@tsb} prompts. Intervals are per metric and not adjusted for multiple comparisons; \cref{tab:deltas_full} alone holds \N{ho@ncomp}. The original \constancy{} has only a partial held-out run (\cref{app:partial}). The paper-versus-code and \yarn{} numbers come from an eight-prompt dev pilot and are descriptive; the mechanism probes use three dev prompts or fewer, and the CPU study of \sega{} uses another latent space. We port \sega{} and \dype{} from their released code with parity tests and ran the official \dype{} code only for \cref{sec:checkpoint}. Finally, a $4096^2$ sample takes a median of \NcompGHcudnnSec\,s on a GH200 GPU (\cref{app:compute}), which limited the number of arms, seeds and resolutions.
