\paragraph{H1 holds on the pooled prompts but not for fresh PickScore}
\tempsb{} is equivalent to \segaofficial{} on the \N{st@h@n@ir} pooled prompts (\cref{fig:fs-confirm}, \cref{tab:strengthen-fresh-tests}). ImageReward differs by \N{st@h@d@ir} \N{st@h@ci@ir} and PickScore by \N{st@h@d@pick} \N{st@h@ci@pick}, both inside their margins with Holm-adjusted equivalence $p=\N{st@h@p@ir}$, so H1 is \N{st@h@H1}. The pool reuses the \N{st@f@n} held-out prompts we had already inspected, so H1 extends the earlier tie rather than confirming it independently. On the \N{st@e@n} fresh prompts alone, PickScore falls from \N{st@em@sega@pick} to \N{st@em@tsb@pick} (\N{st@hf@d@pick} \N{st@hf@ci@pick}). This interval crosses the $-\N{st@h@m@pick}$ margin, so PickScore equivalence on fresh prompts is \N{st@hf@status@pick}; ImageReward stays inside its margin there (\N{st@hf@d@ir} \N{st@hf@ci@ir}).

\paragraph{H2: the allocation's MUSIQ gain replicates}
On the fresh prompts, the allocation raises full-frame MUSIQ from \N{st@em@tsb@musiq} to \N{st@em@sega@musiq} (\N{st@h@d@musiq} \N{st@h@ci@musiq}, Holm $p=\N{st@h@p@musiq}$), so H2 is \N{st@h@H2}. Averaged over seeds 0 to 2 within each held-out prompt, \tempsb{} minus \segaofficial{} is \N{st@rseeds012@d@tsb@ir} \N{st@rseeds012@ci@tsb@ir} on ImageReward and \N{st@rseeds012@d@tsb@musiq} \N{st@rseeds012@ci@tsb@musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ (\cref{tab:strengthen-seeds}). Each seed alone also places the MUSIQ interval below zero. A third hypothesis was withdrawn before generation because its controllers had already lost held out.

\paragraph{Fresh prompts reproduce the held-out ranking}
Released \sega{} keeps its lead on fresh prompts (\cref{tab:strengthen-fresh-arms}). On ImageReward, \ntk{} differs from it by \N{st@e@d@ntk@ir} \N{st@e@ci@ntk@ir} and \segabase{} by \N{st@e@d@segabase@ir} \N{st@e@ci@segabase@ir}. Both temperature arms trail it on fresh PickScore, by \N{st@e@d@tsb@pick} \N{st@e@ci@tsb@pick} on the \sega{} base and \N{st@e@d@ntktemp@pick} \N{st@e@ci@ntktemp@pick} on the \ntk{} base, a gap absent held out. No fresh test shows a reward-model win over \segaofficial.
