\section{Confirmation and controls}\label{sec:confirm}

We registered four further experiments before generating any of their images (prereg\_strengthen.md, amended before generation): a base-by-temperature factorial, a fixed temperature sweep, 150 fresh prompts, and two extra seeds. A block reports a hypothesis only when all of its samples exist. Intervals are nominal 95\% paired prompt-bootstrap intervals (2{,}000 draws, seed 0); $^{*}$ marks a Holm-adjusted $p\le0.05$ inside the block's registered family.

\paragraph{Factorial decomposition}
\Cref{tab:strengthen-factorial} completes the two-by-two design of RoPE base (\ntk{} or \sega) and global logit temperature (none or $s^{0.16}$) on the $\N{st@f@n}$ held-out prompts, and adds the allocation contrast. Averaged over temperature, the \sega{} base changes ImageReward by \N{st@f@d@base@ir} \N{st@f@ci@base@ir} and full-frame MUSIQ by \N{st@f@d@base@musiq} \N{st@f@ci@base@musiq}. Averaged over base, the temperature changes them by \N{st@f@d@temp@ir} \N{st@f@ci@temp@ir} and \N{st@f@d@temp@musiq} \N{st@f@ci@temp@musiq}. The interaction is \N{st@f@d@inter@ir} \N{st@f@ci@inter@ir} on ImageReward and \N{st@f@d@inter@musiq} \N{st@f@ci@inter@musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ. The allocation contrast is the earlier held-out comparison, now tested inside the factorial family: \N{st@f@d@alloc@musiq} \N{st@f@ci@alloc@musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ and \N{st@f@d@alloc@ir} \N{st@f@ci@alloc@ir} on ImageReward. \N{st@f@nholm} of the 80 registered contrast-metric tests survive Holm (\cref{fig:fs-factorial}).

\paragraph{Registered confirmation}
H1 states that \tempsb{} is equivalent to \segaofficial{} on ImageReward (margin $\pm0.082$, half of \segaofficial's held-out gain over \dype) and on PickScore (margin $\pm0.10$). On the pooled 337 prompts the difference is \N{st@h@d@ir} \N{st@h@ci@ir} on ImageReward (Holm $p=\N{st@h@p@ir}$) and \N{st@h@d@pick} \N{st@h@ci@pick} on PickScore (Holm $p=\N{st@h@p@pick}$). H2 states that the allocation raises full-frame MUSIQ on the 150 fresh prompts; the difference is \N{st@h@d@musiq} \N{st@h@ci@musiq} (Holm $p=\N{st@h@p@musiq}$). The registered outcome is \emph{\N{st@h@H1}} for H1 and \emph{\N{st@h@H2}} for H2 (\cref{tab:strengthen-fresh-tests}, \cref{fig:fs-confirm}). The pooled set reuses 187 prompts we had already inspected, so H1 extends rather than independently confirms the earlier tie; the fresh-only estimates appear separately in \cref{fig:fs-confirm}. We withdrew H3 before generation, because its two controllers had already failed with tight intervals.

\paragraph{Seeds}
Averaging seeds 0, 1 and 2 within each held-out prompt ($n=187$ prompts, not 561 images), \tempsb{} minus \segaofficial{} is \N{st@rseeds012@d@tsb@ir} \N{st@rseeds012@ci@tsb@ir} on ImageReward, \N{st@rseeds012@d@tsb@pick} \N{st@rseeds012@ci@tsb@pick} on PickScore and \N{st@rseeds012@d@tsb@musiq} \N{st@rseeds012@ci@tsb@musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ (\cref{tab:strengthen-seeds}). Seeds 1 and 2 alone give \N{st@rseeds12@d@tsb@ir} \N{st@rseeds12@ci@tsb@ir} on ImageReward.
\fi

\ifcsname N@st@done@ckptmatrix\endcsname
\paragraph{Checkpoint at matched guidance}
The dot lattice that led us to \krea{} could have come from guidance, since the earlier comparison ran \fluxdev{} at 3.5 and \krea{} at 4.5. With the official \dype{} code at matched guidance, \fluxdev{} carries \N{st@k@ratio@g3p5}$\times$ \N{st@k@ci@g3p5} the \krea{} lattice power at 3.5 and \N{st@k@ratio@g4p5}$\times$ \N{st@k@ci@g4p5} at 4.5 (8 development prompts, \cref{tab:strengthen-ckpt}). On a bin chosen jointly for all four cells, raising guidance from 3.5 to 4.5 scales the \fluxdev{} lattice by \N{st@k@two@gdev}$\times$ \N{st@k@twoci@gdev} and \krea's by \N{st@k@two@gkrea}$\times$ \N{st@k@twoci@gkrea}. Guidance modulates the lattice, but the checkpoint gap remains at both values. These are development prompts and a descriptive diagnostic.
\fi

\paragraph{Second model family}
The registered Qwen-Image transfer test did not run. All 60 generation tasks failed with CUDA out-of-memory errors while a second process held about 45\,GiB of the same 95\,GiB GPU, and a clean rerun did not fit the remaining compute budget. We therefore make no claim about other model families.
