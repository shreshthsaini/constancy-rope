\paragraph{H1 holds on the pooled prompts but not for fresh PickScore}
\tempsb{} is equivalent to \segaofficial{} on the \N{st@h@n@ir} pooled prompts (\cref{fig:fs-confirm}, \cref{tab:strengthen-fresh-tests}): ImageReward differs by \N{st@h@d@ir} \N{st@h@ci@ir} and PickScore by \N{st@h@d@pick} \N{st@h@ci@pick}, both inside their margins with Holm-adjusted equivalence $p=\N{st@h@p@ir}$, so H1 is \N{st@h@H1}. The margin and the pool were fixed by the amendment of 2026-10-19, before any confirmation image existed; under the original fresh-only $\pm0.05$ rule, the fresh ImageReward interval \N{st@hf@ci@ir} would not have established equivalence. The pool reuses the \N{st@f@n} held-out prompts we had already inspected, so H1 extends the earlier tie rather than confirming it independently. On the \N{st@e@n} fresh prompts alone, PickScore falls from \N{st@em@sega@pick} to \N{st@em@tsb@pick} (\N{st@hf@d@pick} \N{st@hf@ci@pick}), crossing the $-\N{st@h@m@pick}$ margin, so fresh PickScore equivalence is \N{st@hf@status@pick}; fresh ImageReward stays inside the amended margin (\N{st@hf@d@ir}).

\paragraph{H2: the MUSIQ gain replicates}
On the fresh prompts, the allocation raises full-frame MUSIQ from \N{st@em@tsb@musiq} to \N{st@em@sega@musiq} (\N{st@h@d@musiq} \N{st@h@ci@musiq}, Holm $p=\N{st@h@p@musiq}$), so H2 is \N{st@h@H2}. Its PickScore gain there, \N{st@e@d@alloc@pick} \N{st@e@ci@alloc@pick}, is nominal only (Holm $p=\N{st@e@p@alloc@pick}$ over 50 tests), so no reward-model gain survives. Averaged over seeds 0 to 2 within each held-out prompt, \tempsb{} minus \segaofficial{} is \N{st@rseeds012@d@tsb@ir} \N{st@rseeds012@ci@tsb@ir} on ImageReward and \N{st@rseeds012@d@tsb@musiq} \N{st@rseeds012@ci@tsb@musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ (\cref{tab:strengthen-seeds}). Each seed alone also places the MUSIQ interval below zero. A third hypothesis was withdrawn before generation because its controllers had already lost held out.

\paragraph{Fresh prompts reproduce the held-out ranking}
Released \sega{} keeps its lead (\cref{tab:strengthen-fresh-arms}): on ImageReward, \ntk{} differs from it by \N{st@e@d@ntk@ir} and \segabase{} by \N{st@e@d@segabase@ir}, and no fresh test shows a reward-model win over it. Both temperature arms trail it on PickScore, by \N{st@e@d@tsb@pick} \N{st@e@ci@tsb@pick} on the \sega{} base and \N{st@e@d@ntktemp@pick} \N{st@e@ci@ntktemp@pick} on the \ntk{} base, a gap absent held out. \dype{} trades reward for crop quality, held out (\hodci{dype}{ir} ImageReward, \hodci{dype}{musiqc} crop MUSIQ) and fresh. Its crop MUSIQ gain of \N{st@e@d@dype@musiqc} \N{st@e@ci@dype@musiqc} and CLIP-IQA gain of \N{st@e@d@dype@iqa} \N{st@e@ci@dype@iqa} are the only Holm wins over \segaofficial. Its Holm losses of \N{st@e@d@dype@ir} ImageReward and \N{st@e@d@dype@pick} PickScore mean that no single metric orders the methods.
