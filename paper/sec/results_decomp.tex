\subsection{Base and temperature do most of the work}\label{sec:decomp}

\paragraph{The floor and the control}
\Cref{tab:results} lists every held-out arm. \ntk{} keeps \sega's family of frequency base and drops both temperature and allocation; it is far worse on every reward and image-quality metric (ImageReward \hod{ntk}{ir}, full-frame MUSIQ \hod{ntk}{musiq}, crop MUSIQ \hod{ntk}{musiqc}). \tempsb{} keeps \sega's released base and global temperature and drops only the allocation. It ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward (\hodci{tsb}{ir}), PickScore, CLIP and crop MUSIQ, and falls behind on full-frame MUSIQ (\hodci{tsb}{musiq}), CLIP-IQA and the spectral error.

\paragraph{Base and temperature carry the reward-model gain}
Of \sega's gain over \ntk, base and temperature carry \shp{ir} of the ImageReward gain (interval \shci{ir}), \shp{pick} of PickScore, \shp{clip} of CLIP and \shp{musiqc} of crop MUSIQ (\cref{tab:results}, bottom; \cref{fig:hero}a). A share above 100\% means the allocation's point estimate is negative.

\paragraph{The allocation adds full-frame MUSIQ}
The allocation's own contribution, \segaofficial{} minus \tempsb, is \hod{alloc}{musiq} \hoci{alloc}{musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ and \hod{alloc}{iqa} \hoci{alloc}{iqa} on CLIP-IQA. Its intervals cover zero on all three reward models and on crop MUSIQ. The higher full-frame MUSIQ accompanies lower excess luminance power at 8 to 32 pixel wavelengths (\cref{fig:spectrum_main}). This spectral change is consistent with suppressing fine-scale artefacts, but it does not establish what caused MUSIQ to rise, and neither spectral power nor MUSIQ alone measures perceived sharpness.

\paragraph{\dype{} trades reward for crop quality}
Against \segaofficial{} (\cref{fig:forest}), \dype{} is worse on all three reward models (ImageReward \hodci{dype}{ir}, PickScore \hod{dype}{pick}) and better on crop MUSIQ (\hodci{dype}{musiqc}) and CLIP-IQA (\hod{dype}{iqa}). No single metric orders the methods, so we report all of them.
