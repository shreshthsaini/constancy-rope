\subsection{Base and temperature do most of the work}\label{sec:decomp}

\paragraph{Without the allocation, rewards tie and full-frame scores drop}
\Cref{tab:results} lists every held-out arm and \cref{fig:forest} plots its paired differences from \segaofficial. \ntk, which drops both temperature and allocation, is far worse on every reward and image-quality metric: ImageReward falls from \hmean{sega}{ir} for \segaofficial{} to \hmean{ntk}{ir} (\hodci{ntk}{ir}) and full-frame MUSIQ from \hmean{sega}{musiq} to \hmean{ntk}{musiq} (\hodci{ntk}{musiq}). \tempsb, which drops only the allocation, ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward (\hmean{tsb}{ir} against \hmean{sega}{ir}; \hodci{tsb}{ir}), PickScore, CLIP and crop MUSIQ, and falls behind on full-frame MUSIQ (\hmean{tsb}{musiq} against \hmean{sega}{musiq}; \hodci{tsb}{musiq}), CLIP-IQA and the spectral error.

\paragraph{Base and temperature carry the reward-model gain}
Switching \ntk{} to \sega's base and temperature raises ImageReward from \hmean{ntk}{ir} to \hmean{tsb}{ir} (\hodci{basetemp}{ir}), against \hmean{sega}{ir} for \segaofficial. Of \sega's gain over \ntk, base and temperature thus carry \shp{ir} of the ImageReward gain (interval \shci{ir}), \shp{pick} of PickScore, \shp{clip} of CLIP and \shp{musiqc} of crop MUSIQ (\cref{tab:results}, bottom; \cref{fig:hero}a). A share above 100\% means the allocation's point estimate is negative.

\paragraph{The allocation adds full-frame MUSIQ}
Adding the allocation to \tempsb{} raises full-frame MUSIQ from \hmean{tsb}{musiq} to \hmean{sega}{musiq} (\hodci{alloc}{musiq}) and CLIP-IQA from \hmean{tsb}{iqa} to \hmean{sega}{iqa} (\hodci{alloc}{iqa}, not Holm-significant). Its intervals cover zero on all three reward models and on crop MUSIQ. The higher full-frame MUSIQ accompanies lower excess luminance power at 8 to 32 pixel wavelengths (\cref{fig:spectrum_main}). This spectral change is consistent with suppressing fine-scale artefacts, but it does not establish what caused MUSIQ to rise, and neither spectral power nor MUSIQ alone measures perceived sharpness.

\paragraph{\dype{} trades reward for crop quality}
Against \segaofficial{} (\cref{fig:forest}), \dype{} is worse on all three reward models: ImageReward falls from \hmean{sega}{ir} to \hmean{dype}{ir} (\hodci{dype}{ir}) and PickScore by \hoa{dype}{pick}. It is better on crop MUSIQ, which rises from \hmean{sega}{musiqc} to \hmean{dype}{musiqc} (\hodci{dype}{musiqc}), and on CLIP-IQA (\hod{dype}{iqa}). No single metric orders the methods, so we report all of them.
