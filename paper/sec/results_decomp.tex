\section{Results}\label{sec:results}


\subsection{Decomposing \segaofficial: base and temperature do most of the work}\label{sec:decomp}

\Cref{tab:main} and \cref{fig:forest} compare every held-out arm with \segaofficial. Two rows carry the decomposition. \ntk{} keeps \sega's family of frequency base and drops both the temperature and the allocation; it is far worse on every reward and image-quality metric (ImageReward \hod{ntk}{ir}, full-frame MUSIQ \hod{ntk}{musiq}, crop MUSIQ \hod{ntk}{musiqc}). \tempsb{} keeps \sega's released base and global temperature and drops only the allocation. It ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward (\hodci{tsb}{ir}), PickScore, CLIP and crop MUSIQ, and falls behind on full-frame MUSIQ (\hodci{tsb}{musiq}), CLIP-IQA and the spectral error.

\Cref{fig:hero}b and \cref{tab:share} (\cref{app:full}) turn these rows into shares. Of \sega's gain over \ntk, base and temperature carry \shp{ir} of the ImageReward gain (interval \shci{ir}), \shp{pick} of PickScore, \shp{clip} of CLIP and \shp{musiqc} of crop MUSIQ. The allocation's own contribution, \segaofficial{} minus \tempsb, is \hod{alloc}{musiq} \hoci{alloc}{musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ and \hod{alloc}{iqa} \hoci{alloc}{iqa} on CLIP-IQA; its intervals cover zero on all three reward models and on crop MUSIQ. \sega's per-band allocation is therefore real but narrow: it sharpens the full frame as MUSIQ sees it and leaves alignment, preference and crop-level quality where the base and global temperature put them.

\dype{} makes a different trade. Against \segaofficial{} it is worse on all three reward models (ImageReward \hodci{dype}{ir}, PickScore \hod{dype}{pick}) and better on crop MUSIQ (\hodci{dype}{musiqc}) and CLIP-IQA (\hod{dype}{iqa}). No single metric orders the methods, so we report all of them.

\paragraph{Replication at seed 1}
On the \N{so@n@tsb} seed-1 prompts, \tempsb{} again ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward, PickScore and CLIP and loses on full-frame MUSIQ (\sod{tsb}{musiq} \soci{tsb}{musiq}). \N{so@agree} of the \N{so@cells} tie/better/worse verdicts for \tempsb{} and \constancytm{} agree with seed~0 on the same prompts (\cref{tab:seed1}).

\paragraph{Qualitative}
\Cref{fig:qual} (\cref{app:figs}) shows three held-out prompts: \ntk{} loses fine detail, and the other arms differ mainly in native-resolution crops, which 224-pixel scorers never see.
