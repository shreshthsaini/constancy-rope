\section{Results}\label{sec:results}


\subsection{Decomposing \segaofficial: base and temperature do most of the work}\label{sec:decomp}

\Cref{fig:forest} compares every held-out arm with \segaofficial; \cref{tab:main} in \cref{app:full} gives the numerical intervals. Two rows carry the decomposition. \ntk{} keeps \sega's family of frequency base and drops both the temperature and the allocation; it is far worse on every reward and image-quality metric (ImageReward \hod{ntk}{ir}, full-frame MUSIQ \hod{ntk}{musiq}, crop MUSIQ \hod{ntk}{musiqc}). \tempsb{} keeps \sega's released base and global temperature and drops the allocation, with the index-axis difference specified in \cref{sec:methods}. It ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward (\hodci{tsb}{ir}), PickScore, CLIP and crop MUSIQ, and falls behind on full-frame MUSIQ (\hodci{tsb}{musiq}), CLIP-IQA and the spectral error.

\Cref{fig:hero}b shows the paired absolute gains, and \cref{tab:share} (\cref{app:full}) expresses them as shares. Of \sega's gain over \ntk, base and temperature carry \shp{ir} of the ImageReward gain (interval \shci{ir}), \shp{pick} of PickScore, \shp{clip} of CLIP and \shp{musiqc} of crop MUSIQ. The allocation's own contribution, \segaofficial{} minus \tempsb, is \hod{alloc}{musiq} \hoci{alloc}{musiq} on full-frame MUSIQ and \hod{alloc}{iqa} \hoci{alloc}{iqa} on CLIP-IQA; its intervals cover zero on all three reward models and on crop MUSIQ. Its higher full-frame MUSIQ accompanies lower excess luminance power at 8 to 32 pixel wavelengths (\cref{fig:spectrum_main}). This spectral change is consistent with suppressing fine-scale artefacts, but it does not establish what caused MUSIQ to rise. Neither spectral power nor MUSIQ alone measures perceived sharpness.

\dype{} makes a different trade. Against \segaofficial{} it is worse on all three reward models (ImageReward \hodci{dype}{ir}, PickScore \hod{dype}{pick}) and better on crop MUSIQ (\hodci{dype}{musiqc}) and CLIP-IQA (\hod{dype}{iqa}). No single metric orders the methods, so we report all of them.
