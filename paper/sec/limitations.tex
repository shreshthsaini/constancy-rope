\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One model and size.} Only \krea{} at $4096^2$. The registered \qwen{} test ran out of GPU memory, so other backbones, shapes and video are untested.
\item \textbf{Reused prompts and few seeds.} H1 pools the \N{st@f@n} held-out prompts we had already inspected with \N{st@e@n} fresh ones, and on the fresh prompts alone PickScore equivalence is not established (\N{st@hf@d@pick} \N{st@hf@ci@pick}). Two arms have three seeds, most arms one, and all prompts are \afk{} captions.
\item \textbf{Automatic metrics.} The reward models see 224 pixels, MUSIQ and CLIP-IQA are learned proxies, the spectral error uses a mean reference profile, and we ran no human study.
\item \textbf{Multiplicity.} Holm adjustment covers each registered family and, retrospectively, the main paired tables. Intervals are nominal, \cref{tab:deltas_full} alone holds \N{ho@ncomp} comparisons, and no correction spans all exploratory tables.
\item \textbf{Small diagnostic samples.} \constancy{} ran on part of the held-out set; the pilot and the checkpoint and flow-shift controls use eight dev prompts, and mechanism probes at most three.
\item \textbf{Ports.} We port \sega{} and \dype{} with parity tests against released code, ran official \dype{} code only for the checkpoint control, and lack the \sega{} authors' confirmation of our reading of their paper.
\end{itemize}
