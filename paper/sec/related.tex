\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Training-free high-resolution generation}
U-Net methods modify convolutional scale~\citep{he2024scalecrafter,huang2024fouriscale}, fuse patches or scales~\citep{du2024demofusion,qiu2024freescale}, restructure features~\citep{zhang2024hidiffusion} or cascade over frequencies~\citep{zhang2025frecas}. For flow transformers~\citep{peebles2023dit,esser2024sd3,flux2024}, I-Max and HiFlow follow a low-resolution trajectory~\citep{du2024imax,bu2025hiflow}. HRDiT adapts pretrained transformers~\citep{hrdit2026} and Diffusion-4K fine-tunes at 4K~\citep{zhang2025diffusion4k}; we study embedding edits that add neither training nor sampling passes.

\paragraph{Rotary extrapolation and attention temperature}
Interpolation, NTK scaling, YaRN and LongRoPE remap rotary frequencies~\citep{chen2023pi,bloc97ntk,peng2024yarn,ding2024longrope}, and frequency analyses separate positional and semantic roles~\citep{barbero2025round,xiong2025dope}. In diffusion, RIFLEx targets video repetition~\citep{zhao2025riflex}, \dype{} schedules remapping~\citep{issachar2025dype}, UltraImage selects frequencies~\citep{ultraimage2025}, TIDE controls temperature~\citep{tide2026}, \sega{} adds per-band gains~\citep{sega2026} and AReA adapts locality~\citep{area2026}. Log-key-count temperature is established in variable-size diffusion and Scalable-Softmax~\citep{jin2023training,nakanishi2025ssmax}; we isolate its contribution from the base and the allocation. Spectral analyses of diffusion~\citep{dieleman2024spectral,falck2025fourier,kadkhodaie2024generalization,graikos2026jacobian} motivate interventions that target spectra~\citep{scg2026}, weight training by contrast sensitivity~\citep{csflow2026} or edit cross-attention Fourier bands~\citep{oh2026afm}.
