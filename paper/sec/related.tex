\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Training-free high-resolution generation}
U-Net methods dilate convolutions in space~\citep{he2024scalecrafter} or frequency~\citep{huang2024fouriscale}, fuse patches~\citep{du2024demofusion} or scales~\citep{qiu2024freescale}, restructure features~\citep{zhang2024hidiffusion} or cascade over frequencies~\citep{zhang2025frecas}. For rectified-flow transformers~\citep{peebles2023dit,esser2024sd3,flux2024}, I-Max~\citep{du2024imax} and HiFlow~\citep{bu2025hiflow} guide the high-resolution trajectory with a low-resolution one, HRDiT~\citep{hrdit2026} adapts pretrained transformers, and Diffusion-4K~\citep{zhang2025diffusion4k} fine-tunes at 4K and supplies our prompts. We study the single-pass, embedding-level family, which changes no weights and adds no passes.

\paragraph{Rotary extrapolation and attention temperature}
Language models remap rotary frequencies by interpolation~\citep{chen2023pi}, NTK-aware scaling~\citep{bloc97ntk}, YaRN~\citep{peng2024yarn} or LongRoPE~\citep{ding2024longrope}; \citet{barbero2025round} and DoPE~\citep{xiong2025dope} analyse which frequencies carry position and which carry semantics. For diffusion transformers, RIFLEx~\citep{zhao2025riflex} lowers the frequency that causes repetition in long videos, \dype{}~\citep{issachar2025dype} schedules the remapping with the noise level, UltraImage~\citep{ultraimage2025} revisits frequency selection, TIDE~\citep{tide2026} adds step-aware temperature control, \sega~\citep{sega2026} adds per-band gains, and AReA~\citep{area2026}, from \sega's authors, adapts attention locality. Scaling logits by the log of the key count keeps attention entropy roughly constant, as in variable-size diffusion~\citep{jin2023training} and Scalable-Softmax~\citep{nakanishi2025ssmax}; our results suggest that this knob, together with the base, does most of the work at $4096^2$.

\paragraph{Spectral views and efficient coding}
Diffusion denoises roughly coarse to fine~\citep{dieleman2024spectral,falck2025fourier}, and denoisers adapt to image structure through harmonic bases~\citep{kadkhodaie2024generalization,graikos2026jacobian}. Spectral guidance steers toward target spectra~\citep{scg2026}, CSFlow weights flow matching by contrast sensitivity~\citep{csflow2026}, and Attention Frequency Modulation edits the Fourier bands of cross-attention~\citep{oh2026afm}. Our controllers act on the rotary bands of self-attention and draw on natural-image spectra~\citep{field1987relations,dong1995statistics}, human sensitivity~\citep{knill1990human,parraga2000human}, efficient coding~\citep{atick1990towards,atick1992retina,vanhateren1992theory}, contrast constancy~\citep{georgeson1975contrast} and blur adaptation by divisive gain control~\citep{webster2002neural,heeger1992normalization,schwartz2001natural,carandini2012normalization}.
