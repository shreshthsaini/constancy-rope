\paragraph{Model and sampler}
All comparisons except one checkpoint control use the \krea{} transformer~\citep{flux1kreadev2025}, the checkpoint loaded by the official \dype{} and \sega{} scripts, with the \fluxdev{} text encoders and autoencoder~\citep{flux2024}. A $4096^2$ image has $\N{c@ntgt}$ tokens, \N{c@tokratio} times the \N{c@ntrain} of a native $1024^2$ image ($s=\N{c@s}$ per side). Every arm uses \N{c@steps} Euler flow-matching steps, distilled guidance 4.5, bfloat16, batch size one, the same per-prompt seeds and the capped flow shift $\mu=\N{c@muofficial}$ (\cref{app:impl}).

\paragraph{Prompts and split}
Of the 195 evaluation captions of \afk{}~\citep{zhang2025diffusion4k}, rows 0--7 form a dev set for every design choice and rows 8--194 the held-out set ($n=\N{ho@nprompts}$). We fixed this split after an eight-prompt pilot and before inspecting any held-out score; the decomposition hypothesis arose from that pilot and was tested only held out. Seed~0 is used unless stated. An early seed-1 replication (\cref{app:seed1}) and the partial \constancy{} run use the manifest's first rows, not random subsamples.

\paragraph{Arms and baselines}
Six arms ran on all held-out prompts: \ntk, \dype, \segaofficial, \tempsb, \constancytm{} and \segactm{} (\cref{tab:knobs,tab:results}). \constancy{} ran on \hon{cons} of them, and \segapaper{} and the \aliastemp{} variants only on dev prompts (\cref{app:partial}).

\paragraph{Registered confirmation}
After the held-out analysis, we registered four experiments and their Holm families before generating any image (\cref{app:confirm}). The \emph{factorial} adds \ntktemp{} and \segabase{} on the held-out prompts (\cref{tab:strengthen-factorial}; 8 contrasts on 10 metrics, family of 80). The \emph{sweep} runs \tempsb{} at five multipliers fixed in advance (1, 1.1, $s^{0.16}$, $4/3$, 1.45) on rows 8--62 (family of 40). Because the held-out prompts had been inspected, the \emph{fresh set} draws \N{st@e@n} unused \afk{} training captions by a seeded permutation, screened from text alone; six arms ran on it (family of 50). The \emph{seed} block adds seeds 1 and 2 for \segaofficial{} and \tempsb{} on the held-out prompts, averaged within prompt (family of 10). Two hypotheses decide the outcome under Holm over three tests. H1 holds if \tempsb{} matches \segaofficial{} within $\pm\N{st@h@m@ir}$ ImageReward, half the held-out gain of \segaofficial{} over \dype, and $\pm\N{st@h@m@pick}$ PickScore on the \N{st@h@n@ir} pooled prompts. A dated amendment of 2026-10-19, made before any confirmation image existed, set this margin and pool. The original registration required $\pm0.05$ ImageReward on the fresh prompts alone, which the fresh interval \N{st@hf@ci@ir} would not have met. H2 holds if the allocation raises full-frame MUSIQ on the fresh prompts. Two dev controls (checkpoint, flow shift) are descriptive.

\paragraph{Hyperparameter selection}
From the second campaign on, a written gate governed the dev set. A variant could leave it only with higher ImageReward and full-frame MUSIQ than \segaofficial{} on the eight dev prompts and a CLIP drop of at most 0.3. At most two variants could advance per campaign. No setting was chosen from held-out or fresh results. Eight prompts reject only clearly worse variants: \tempsb{} looked slightly better than \segaofficial{} on dev MUSIQ yet trails by \hoa{tsb}{musiq} points held out. Rerunning \segaofficial{} moved dev MUSIQ by more than the margin of the variant that passed the third gate (\cref{app:dev}).

\paragraph{Metrics}
\emph{Alignment and preference}: CLIP score~\citep{radford2021clip,hessel2021clipscore} ($100\max(\cos,0)$ of ViT-L/14 embeddings), PickScore~\citep{kirstain2023pickapic} and ImageReward~\citep{xu2023imagereward}, which resize their input to 224 pixels and so measure layout and alignment. \emph{No-reference quality}: MUSIQ~\citep{ke2021musiq} and CLIP-IQA~\citep{wang2023clipiqa} on the full $4096^2$ frame (``full'') and averaged over four fixed $1024^2$ crops (``crop''). Full-frame MUSIQ (pyiqa 0.1.16) scores all 16,577 patches of the native $32^2$ grid and its 224- and 384-pixel views (\cref{app:impl}), so it weights context and scale differently from crop MUSIQ. \emph{Spectral fidelity}: RMS and slope errors of luminance power in 11 octave bands against the mean profile of the 195 \afk{} references, and a tiling index (\cref{app:full}). No metric measures 4K quality alone, FID and KID~\citep{heusel2017fid,binkowski2018kid} are too noisy at our sample sizes to rank methods, and we ran no human study.

\paragraph{Statistics}
Each comparison is a paired difference over the same prompts and seed, with a 95\% percentile bootstrap interval over 2,000 prompt resamples. Better, worse and tie describe these nominal intervals; a tie means that the interval covers zero, not equivalence, which only H1 tests. Approximate two-sided centered-bootstrap $p$-values receive Holm correction within each registered family and, retrospectively, within \cref{tab:results,tab:main} and within \cref{tab:deltas_full}. A star ($^{*}$) marks an adjusted $p\le0.05$; intervals and triangles stay nominal. The confirmation analyses resample prompts in manifest order, so an interval endpoint can differ from earlier tables in its last digit.

\paragraph{Compute}
The \NcompNimgs{} retained $4096^2$ images of the main campaigns took \NcompGPUhours{} GPU-hours (\NcompGPUhoursGH{} on GH200, \NcompGPUhoursBW{} on GB200). A held-out image takes a median \NcompGHcudnnSec\,s on one GH200 GPU and \NcompBWSec\,s on one GB200. Forcing FlashAttention-2 makes a sample take \NcompFlashRatio{} times as long, more than any method difference (\cref{tab:cost}), so timing comparisons must fix kernel and GPU.
