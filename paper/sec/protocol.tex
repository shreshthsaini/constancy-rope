\section{Experimental setup}\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Model and sampler}
The held-out comparisons use the \krea{} transformer~\citep{flux1kreadev2025}, the checkpoint loaded by the official \dype{} and \sega{} scripts, with the \fluxdev{} text encoders and autoencoder~\citep{flux2024}. A $4096^2$ image has $\N{c@ntgt}$ tokens, \N{c@tokratio} times the \N{c@ntrain} of a native $1024^2$ image ($s=\N{c@s}$ per side). Every held-out arm uses \N{c@steps} Euler flow-matching steps, distilled guidance 4.5, bfloat16, batch size one, the same per-prompt seeds and the capped flow shift $\mu=\N{c@muofficial}$ (\cref{app:impl}).

\paragraph{Prompts and split}
Of the 195 evaluation captions of \afk{}~\citep{zhang2025diffusion4k}, rows 0--7 form a development set for variant and hyperparameter choices and rows 8--194 the held-out set ($n=\N{ho@nprompts}$). We fixed this split after an eight-prompt pilot and before inspecting any held-out score; the decomposition hypothesis arose from that pilot and was tested only held out. Seed~0 is used throughout. A seed-1 replication covers rows 8--62 ($n=\N{so@n@tsb}$) for \segaofficial, \tempsb{} and \constancytm; it and the partial \constancy{} run use the first rows of the fixed manifest, not random subsamples.

\paragraph{Arms and baselines}
Six arms ran on all held-out prompts: \ntk, \dype, \segaofficial, \tempsb, \constancytm{} and \segactm{} (\cref{tab:knobs}). The original \constancy{} ran on \hon{cons} held-out prompts, and \segapaper{} and the \aliastemp{} variants only on dev prompts (\cref{app:partial}).

\paragraph{Hyperparameter selection}
From the second campaign on, a written gate let a variant leave the dev set only with higher ImageReward and full-frame MUSIQ than \segaofficial{} on the eight dev prompts and a CLIP drop of at most 0.3, and at most two variants could advance per campaign. Dev numbers are selection evidence only (\cref{app:dev}).

\paragraph{Metrics}
\emph{Alignment and preference}: CLIP score~\citep{radford2021clip,hessel2021clipscore} ($100\max(\cos,0)$ of ViT-L/14 embeddings), PickScore~\citep{kirstain2023pickapic} and ImageReward~\citep{xu2023imagereward}. These models resize their input to 224 pixels, so we read them as measures of layout and alignment. \emph{No-reference quality}: MUSIQ~\citep{ke2021musiq} and CLIP-IQA~\citep{wang2023clipiqa} on the full $4096^2$ frame (``full'') and averaged over four fixed $1024^2$ crops (``crop''). Full-frame MUSIQ (pyiqa 0.1.16) scores all 16,577 patches of the native $32^2$ grid and its 224- and 384-pixel views with no cap (\cref{app:impl}), so it includes every original pixel but weights context and scale differently from crop MUSIQ. \emph{Spectral fidelity}: RMS and slope errors of luminance power in 11 octave bands from $2^{-12}$ to $2^{-1}$ cycles per pixel against the mean profile of the 195 \afk{} references, and a tiling index (\cref{app:full}). FID and KID~\citep{heusel2017fid,binkowski2018kid} are too noisy at our sample sizes to rank methods. No metric measures 4K quality alone, and we ran no human study.

\paragraph{Statistics}
Each comparison is a paired difference against \segaofficial{} over the same prompts and seed, with a 95\% percentile bootstrap interval over 2,000 prompt resamples. Better, worse and tie describe these nominal intervals; a tie means the interval covers zero, not equivalence. We also compute approximate two-sided centered-bootstrap $p$-values and apply Holm correction separately over the condensed comparisons of \cref{tab:results,tab:main} and over all comparisons of \cref{tab:deltas_full}; stars mark adjusted $p\le0.05$, while intervals and triangles stay nominal. The allocation's full-frame MUSIQ gain survives both families, but its CLIP-IQA gain and \segactm's small CLIP loss do not. These bootstrap tests give approximate error control, and the other tables are descriptive.

\paragraph{Compute}
The \NcompNimgs{} retained $4096^2$ images took \NcompGPUhours{} GPU-hours (\NcompGPUhoursGH{} on GH200, \NcompGPUhoursBW{} on GB200); a held-out image takes a median \NcompGHcudnnSec\,s on one GH200 GPU and \NcompBWSec\,s on one GB200 (\cref{app:compute}).
