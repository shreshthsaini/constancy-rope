\begin{abstract}
Training-free rotary position embedding (RoPE) edits let diffusion transformers generate $4096\times4096$ images, but comparisons of complete methods obscure each edit's contribution. On \krea, over \N{ho@nprompts} held-out prompts with paired bootstrap intervals, \sega's base and global temperature recover all of its ImageReward gain over an NTK base (\shp{ir}, interval \shci{ir}). They tie the released method on three reward models and crop MUSIQ; ``tie'' means that the difference interval covers zero, not equivalence. The allocation adds \hoa{alloc}{musiq} full-frame MUSIQ points alongside reduced excess fine-scale power, without establishing a causal link. Of four controllers derived from efficient coding and contrast constancy, the two evaluated fully held out tie or lose to released \sega{} on every reward and image-quality metric. Small trajectory diagnostics suggest possible explanations. Claims for spectral or content-adaptive RoPE edits should be tested against a base-plus-temperature control with full-resolution metrics.
\end{abstract}
