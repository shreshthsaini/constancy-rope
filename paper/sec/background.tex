\paragraph{RoPE in FLUX}
FLUX~\citep{flux2024} packs the autoencoder latent into $2\times2$ patches, so one token covers $16\times16$ pixels and a $4096^2$ image has a $\N{c@tokside}^2$ token grid ($64^2$ at $1024^2$). Each 128-dimensional attention head has rotary blocks of 16, 56 and 56 dimensions for an index axis and the two spatial axes. Pair $j$ of spatial axis $a$ rotates at $\omega_{a,j}=\theta^{-2j/D}$ with $\theta=10^4$ and $D=56$, so wavelengths $2\pi/\omega_{a,j}$ run from about 6 to about 45{,}000 tokens over the 28 pairs.

\paragraph{The logit as a Fourier series over the offset}
Write the query and key coefficients of pair $j$ on axis $a$ as complex numbers $\tilde q_{a,j}$ and $\tilde k_{a,j}$, and let a method multiply both by a gain $m_{a,j}$ after the rotation, with which it commutes. The logit between a query at position $u$ and a key at position $v$ is then
\begin{equation}
\ell_{uv}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_k}}\sum_{a,\,j} g_{a,j}\,\bigl|\tilde q_{a,j}\bigr|\bigl|\tilde k_{a,j}\bigr|\cos\!\bigl(\omega'_{a,j}\Delta^{a}_{uv}+\phi_{a,j}\bigr),\qquad g_{a,j}=m_{a,j}^{2},
\label{eq:fourier}
\end{equation}
where $d_k=128$ is the head dimension, $\Delta^{a}_{uv}=p^{a}_u-p^{a}_v$ is the offset along axis $a$, $\omega'_{a,j}$ is the frequency after extrapolation and $\phi_{a,j}$ is the content phase. The logit is thus a Fourier series in the offset, one term per rotary pair, plus a non-rotating index-axis part. Content sets magnitudes and phases; extrapolation changes only the frequencies $\omega'_{a,j}$ and the gains $g_{a,j}$. A gain shared by all pairs, $g\equiv\tau$, is an inverse temperature, and a $j$-dependent gain reweights the bands of the kernel.

\paragraph{Three knobs}
We sort methods by the knobs they turn (\cref{tab:knobs,fig:method}). \emph{Frequency base}: at $4096^2$ the offsets reach $s=\N{c@s}$ times the native range, so slow pairs see phases never seen in training. Position interpolation divides every frequency by $s$~\citep{chen2023pi}; NTK-aware scaling raises the base to $\theta'=\theta s^{D/(D-2)}$, which leaves the fastest pair alone and divides the slowest by $s$~\citep{bloc97ntk}; YaRN interpolates only the slow pairs~\citep{peng2024yarn}. \emph{Global temperature}: a shared gain $\tau_N=\log N/\log N_{\mathrm{train}}$ offsets the growth of softmax entropy with the key count $N$~\citep{jin2023training}, and $\tau_N=\N{c@taulogn}$ at $4096^2$. \emph{Per-band gain}: a $j$-dependent $g_{a,j}$ that may vary with the noise level and the image.

\paragraph{The flow schedule at 4K}
FLUX samples a rectified flow~\citep{lipman2023flow,liu2023flow} with the time shift $\sigma'=e^{\mu}/\bigl(e^{\mu}+1/\sigma-1\bigr)$, where $\sigma$ is the unshifted noise level and $\mu$ grows linearly with the token count~\citep{esser2024sd3}. The diffusers default~\citep{vonplaten2022diffusers} extrapolates that line to $\mu=\N{c@mudefault}$ at $4096^2$, so the last evaluated noise level is $\sigma=\N{c@sigmalastdefault}$: every model call sees almost pure noise, and the final Euler step jumps from there to the image. The official \dype{} and \sega{} pipelines cap $\mu$ at $\N{c@muofficial}$ (last $\sigma=\N{c@sigmalastofficial}$), as do all our arms. This diagnosis is numerical, since we did not measure the default's effect on image quality, but comparisons that mix the two schedules confound position embedding with sampling.
