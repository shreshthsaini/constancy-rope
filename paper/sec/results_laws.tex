\subsection{Principled per-band laws do not beat \segaofficial}\label{sec:laws}

\paragraph{Neither held-out law improves on \segaofficial}
\constancytm{} ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward and PickScore (\cref{tab:results}, middle block). It is worse on CLIP (\hod{ctm}{clip}), full-frame MUSIQ (\hmean{ctm}{musiq} against \hmean{sega}{musiq}; \hodci{ctm}{musiq}), crop MUSIQ (\hod{ctm}{musiqc}), CLIP-IQA and the spectral error. Its one gain is a lower tiling index (\hod{ctm}{tile}). \segactm{} at $\eta=1$, the only variant to pass the campaign-2 dev gate, ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward (\hodci{sctm}{ir}) and full-frame MUSIQ (\hodci{sctm}{musiq}). It loses on PickScore (\hodci{sctm}{pick}) and crop MUSIQ (\hmean{sctm}{musiqc} against \hmean{sega}{musiqc}; \hodci{sctm}{musiqc}), and its CLIP interval, \hoci{sctm}{clip}, ends just below zero. \aliastemp{} and \aliastemps{} failed the dev gate on ImageReward (\cref{tab:dev}).

\paragraph{Against their own controls the corrections cost crop quality}
\Cref{tab:alloc} adds each allocation to the same base and temperature. \constancytm{} lowers crop MUSIQ from \hmean{tsb}{musiqc} for \tempsb{} to \hmean{ctm}{musiqc} (\hodci{ctmtsb}{musiqc}) and full-frame MUSIQ from \hmean{tsb}{musiq} to \hmean{ctm}{musiq} (\hodci{ctmtsb}{musiq}), while slightly improving both spectral errors (\hod{ctmtsb}{spec} and \hod{ctmtsb}{slope}). On top of \sega's allocation, the correction keeps most of the full-frame MUSIQ gain but lowers crop MUSIQ to \hmean{sctm}{musiqc} (\hodci{sctmtsb}{musiqc}). \Cref{fig:qual_main} in \cref{app:figs} shows native crops of all six arms. Four mechanism diagnostics suggest explanations for these losses but identify no causal mediator (\cref{app:mech}).
