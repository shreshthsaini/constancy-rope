\paragraph{Neither held-out law improves on \segaofficial}
In \cref{tab:results} (middle block), \constancytm{} ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward and PickScore and is worse on CLIP (\hod{ctm}{clip}), full-frame MUSIQ (\hmean{ctm}{musiq} against \hmean{sega}{musiq}; \hodci{ctm}{musiq}), crop MUSIQ (\hod{ctm}{musiqc}), CLIP-IQA and the spectral error; its one gain is a lower tiling index (\hod{ctm}{tile}). \segactm{} at $\eta=1$, the only variant to pass the campaign-2 dev gate, ties \segaofficial{} on ImageReward (\hodci{sctm}{ir}) and full-frame MUSIQ (\hodci{sctm}{musiq}). It loses on PickScore (\hodci{sctm}{pick}) and crop MUSIQ (\hmean{sctm}{musiqc} against \hmean{sega}{musiqc}; \hodci{sctm}{musiqc}), and its CLIP interval, \hoci{sctm}{clip}, ends just below zero. \aliastemp{} and \aliastemps{} failed the dev gate on ImageReward (\cref{tab:dev}).

\paragraph{Against their own controls the corrections cost crop quality}
\Cref{tab:alloc} adds each allocation to the same base and temperature. \constancytm{} lowers crop MUSIQ from \hmean{tsb}{musiqc} for \tempsb{} to \hmean{ctm}{musiqc} (\hodci{ctmtsb}{musiqc}) and full-frame MUSIQ from \hmean{tsb}{musiq} to \hmean{ctm}{musiq} (\hodci{ctmtsb}{musiq}) while slightly improving both spectral errors (\hod{ctmtsb}{spec} and \hod{ctmtsb}{slope}). Adding the correction on top of \sega's allocation keeps most of its full-frame MUSIQ gain but still lowers crop MUSIQ to \hmean{sctm}{musiqc} (\hodci{sctmtsb}{musiqc}). \Cref{fig:qual_main} shows illustrative native crops of all six arms on three held-out prompts; compositions differ between arms.

\paragraph{Diagnostics suggest explanations, not mediators}
Four diagnostics identify no causal mediator of image quality (\cref{fig:mechanism}, \cref{app:mech}). The late-profile visibility gate opens only after the first $\NmechConstOnsetStepMed$ of $\NmechSteps$ steps (median, \NmechConstN{} held-out prompts); a time-matched target activates the correction from step $\NmechTmOnsetStepMed$, but its better spectral match accompanies lower MUSIQ against the \tempsb{} control. The released allocation is mostly a fixed tilt whose prompt-dependent remainder, which includes each prompt's noise draw, only bounds content dependence; \segactm{} enlarges that remainder from $\NmechSegaContentPct\%$ to $\NmechCtmContentPct\%$ of the variance without a held-out quality gain. Three dev probes find weak, prompt-dependent, cross-band responses, and a two-prompt local linear model has a median error of $\NmechCrSeventeenErrMed$ relative to predicting no change; these probes question band-local control without establishing a universal limit on it.
