\section{Additional figures}\label{app:figs}

This appendix expands \cref{tab:results,fig:spectrum_main} and holds the development figures behind \cref{sec:checkpoint,sec:papercode}. \Cref{fig:forest} plots every held-out paired difference, \cref{fig:qual_main,fig:qual} show held-out samples, \cref{fig:spectrum} the octave-band spectra, \cref{fig:papercode,tab:papercode_scores} the development paper-versus-code comparison and \cref{fig:checkpoint} the development checkpoint diagnostic.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\figorplaceholder[\linewidth]{figs/f3_forest.pdf}{2.6in}
\caption{\textbf{Paired differences from \segaofficial.} No per-band law improves on \segaofficial{} in any reward or image-quality metric, and \dype's isolated gains in crop MUSIQ and CLIP-IQA come with losses elsewhere. The dashed blue line is \segaofficial; marks show held-out paired differences with 95\% bootstrap intervals (rows 8--194, seed 0, $n=\N{ho@nprompts}$), pale where the interval includes 0, and \cref{tab:main} lists them. Hollow marks: seed-1 replication on rows 8--62 ($n=\N{so@n@tsb}$) for \tempsb{} and \constancytm{} (none for \segactm); triangles: \ntk{} beyond the axis limit; arrows: better direction.}
\label{fig:forest}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\figorplaceholder[\linewidth]{figs/f4_qualitative_main.pdf}{3.26in}
\caption{\textbf{Native crops show what thumbnails hide.} Held-out rows 8, 9 and 10 (left to right), the first three held-out prompts, were fixed before any output was viewed; seed 0, each Lanczos-reduced full frame beside its unaltered 384-pixel crop (red box). During review, we chose one shared crop location per row on a fixed grid by maximizing the minimum Laplacian energy across all six arms, each normalized by its 90th percentile over candidate locations. This output-dependent rule favors detail present in every arm and selects no winning method; differing compositions remain a limitation. Full prompts are in \cref{fig:qual}.}
\label{fig:qual_main}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\figorplaceholder[\linewidth]{figs/f8_spectrum.pdf}{2.2in}
\caption{\textbf{Spectra against references and \sega.} Without \sega's per-band allocation, luminance power at 8 to 32 pixel wavelengths (gray band) sits above released \sega's; \constancytm{} leaves a similar excess, and \ntk{} falls short from 8 to 1024 pixels. (a)~Log-power error against the mean $\ln P$ of the 195 \afk{} references, per octave band of linear-sRGB luminance; (b)~paired difference from \segaofficial, the dashed blue zero line; positive means more power. Held-out rows 8--194 ($n=\N{ho@nprompts}$, seed 0) with 95\% bootstrap intervals over prompts; the dashed vertical marks the Nyquist frequency of a 224-pixel view.}
\label{fig:spectrum}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]

Prompts, left to right: ``A stone bridge with arched spans reflects golden light on the water, flanked by buildings and a leafy tree under a clear blue sky.'' ``A panoramic view of Paris featuring the Eiffel Tower, the Seine River, and various ornate buildings with greenery and bustling activity, including boats on the water and crowds along walkways.'' ``A cluttered artist's corner features an ornate gold frame, a painting of a man with glasses, several empty picture frames, a vintage suitcase, and various brushes in a tin can, all set against a textured gray wall and draped with patterned fabric.''}
