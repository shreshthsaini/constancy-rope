# Figure style reference (constancy_rope)

2026-10-19. Surveyed the figures of twelve papers (SEGA, AReA and the two user-named papers as primary references, then Looped-DiT, LiFT, BudgetPix, Seedance 1.0, Unified Latents, GenCeption, DyPE and UltraImage); pages rendered at 110 dpi, words and font sizes counted from vector text with PyMuPDF, raster text counted by eye.
Sources: analyst notes in `/scratch/09032/saini_2/tmp/style_refs/notes/`, page renders in `style_refs/<name>/pages/` and `style_refs/user_refs_render/`, plot word counts measured for this file from vector text, and, for reference-list members not rendered here, `Projects/Exploration/Stationary_Forcing/logs/design/style/STYLE_GUIDE.md` ("the guide") and `DESIGN_NOTES.md`.

**Standing instruction from the user.** Whenever we write a paper, its figures must meet or exceed the bar these references set, and the text inside figures stays minimal: names, symbols and axis numbers only, never a sentence. Explanation goes in the caption. Rules R1 to R10 below turn this into numbers, and `CRITIQUE_v2.md` applies them figure by figure.

Name resolutions. "Left loop diffusion transformer": LiFT (2610.05538), because speech-to-text hears "LiFT" as "left" and LiFT describes itself as a shared DiT core applied in a loop; Looped-DiT (2609.40305) is the literal title match, so both are surveyed and every rule below holds for both. "Budget picks": BudgetPix (2610.12307); the analyst verified its title and all seven authors. ByteDance: Seedance 1.0 (2506.09113), the newest ByteDance Seed report with a method diagram, plots and comparison grids. DeepMind: Unified Latents (2602.17270), with GenCeption (2607.09024) as the second paper.

## 4. Consolidated style guide for our paper

**R1. Words inside figures.** No sentence, clause or qualifier inside artwork (sample sizes, seeds, "illustrative", "this pair only", row IDs); every label is a name, symbol or number of 1 to 3 words. English words per figure (math excluded): hero at most 20; method diagram 15 to 30 label tokens counting math labels, of which at most 15 English words; plot figure at most 6 per panel for axis titles, panel names and annotations, plus one legend or label set of at most 3 words per series; image grid at most 3 words per label and 12 label words in all, plus one prompt excerpt of at most 6 words per example.
Evidence: heroes SEGA p1 11, AReA p1 15, BudgetPix p1 16, MFlow p1 0; diagrams LILIE Fig 3 p2 about 14 and Fig 4 p4 about 25 (the skill's 15 to 30), guide rule 1 (10 to 55), UL Fig 1 p3 15; plots Looped-DiT Figs 6 and 7 (pp. 7, 9) 14 and 15 words over 4 panels, BudgetPix Fig 7 p8 34 over 10 panels; grids BudgetPix Fig 6 p7 6 words, LILIE prompt bands.

**R2. Font sizes (body 10 pt).** One sans family: Nimbus Sans in plots, TeX Gyre Heros in TikZ. Tick labels 7 pt (0.7). Axis titles, legend entries, method, row and column labels, panel names, diagram tags and prompt excerpts 8 pt (0.8). Math labels 7 to 8 pt. Panel letters with names 9 pt bold. Floor 7 pt for every glyph; small multiples with five or more panels per row may set titles and row labels at 7 pt.
Evidence: skill (ticks 7 to 8 pt, axis titles 8 to 9, panel titles 9, never under 6); BudgetPix Fig 7 p8 (6.9 to 7.9 pt over 10 panels); guide rule 8; Looped-DiT Fig 2 p2 (8 pt block labels). SEGA's 4.5 pt prompts (p7), AReA's 3.75 to 3.9 pt tags (pp. 1, 7) and MFlow Fig 7's 5.7 pt labels sit below this floor.

**R3. Palette.** At most 3 hue families plus grey in a diagram and 4 plus grey in a plot; a light and a dark shade of one hue count once (sky and cobalt are one family). Method colours come only from the `DESIGN_NOTES.md` table, with identical hex values in every figure and table. Red #FF0000 and cyan #00B0F0 are reserved for crop boxes. A diagram block is filled only to mark its role, with a 15 to 25% tint of its border hue, and at most two roles are filled; no decorative highlight boxes. Text is neutral ink #1F2329 unless the label names marks of its own colour in the same figure.
Evidence: guide rule 3 (Looped-DiT Fig 2 p2, AReA Fig 4 p4, BudgetPix Fig 3 p4 use 2 to 3 hues); BudgetPix Fig 7 p8 (four hues plus a grey base); skill (four hues at most, one mapping across all 20 LILIE figures, fills as 15 to 25% tints, LILIE Fig 3 p2).

**R4. Plots.** L spines at 0.6 pt, outward ticks, light horizontal gridlines at 0.4 pt only. At most 3 ticks per axis on small multiples, 5 to 8 on a single panel. No printed data values on interval plots, no off-axis value tags and no tables inside figures; a bar chart, if ever used, carries values over the bars at 7 pt with the best bold. One short feature annotation per panel in axis units is allowed ("25 px"). One legend per figure: an unframed strip above the panels, or direct labels; never a framed legend inside axes. Direction as ↑ or ↓ after each metric name, never also as shading. Every estimate shows its nominal 95% paired bootstrap interval: a 1.0 pt whisker without caps for points, an 18% band for curves; the reference arm is a 0.6 pt zero line labelled once.
Evidence: AReA Figs 3, 11, 16 (L spines, 0.4 pt grids, inline reference labels); Looped-DiT Figs 3, 6, 7 and BudgetPix Fig 7 p8 (about 3 ticks on small multiples, metric with arrow, one legend strip); skill (6 to 8 ticks on full plots, unframed top legend, values over bars); guide rules 4 and 6; SEGA Fig 7 p16 and BudgetPix Figs 7, 8, 10 print no values; AReA Fig 14 p24 (bands at 14 to 18%). The references mostly show no uncertainty; we keep intervals because every claim is a paired difference (DESIGN_NOTES).

**R5. Panel labels.** "(a) Name": the letter in parentheses and a name of 1 to 3 words, 9 pt bold, at the top-left outside the panel on a shared baseline; the caption addresses panels by letter. Only figures whose panels differ in kind get letters (F1, F2, A.2, A.3, A.4, F.1). Small multiples (F3), single plots (F4) and image grids (F5, A.1) get none, and their captions use italic Top, Bottom, Left or Right.
Evidence: skill and LILIE Fig 3 p2 ("(a) Encoder Pretraining"); Looped-DiT Figs 1 and 3 (bold "(a) Title"); guide rule 2; AReA Fig 17 p28. No reference uses a bare letter; SEGA, AReA and BudgetPix Fig 6 leave single-kind figures unlettered.

**R6. Image grids and crops.** Equal square tiles, no borders, 0.03 in gutters inside a group and 0.10 in between groups. Method labels in one line at 8 pt neutral ink under the bottom row, the paired reference SEGA (released) in bold, our per-band laws last (F1 alone puts them above, because its result strip sits below). One prompt excerpt (at most 6 words, italic 8 pt) on a grey band (#F3F4F6, 0.14 in) above each example; row IDs and full prompts go to the caption. Crops: a 1.0 pt square box on the full frame, red #FF0000 for the first crop and cyan #00B0F0 for the second; the native-pixel crop is its own tile of the same width directly below its full frame (beside it in F1), framed 1.0 pt in the box colour; no halos, connectors, letters or per-example scores.
Evidence: SEGA Fig 5 p7 (2 to 3 pt gutters, labels under the bottom row, ours last); AReA Fig 5 p7 and DyPE Fig 4 p6 (labels below); LiFT Fig 7 p13 (2 and 10 pt gaps); skill and LILIE (prompt excerpts in grey bands, column labels at the bottom); MFlow Figs 22 and 23 (red 1 pt box, crop directly below at the same width); SEGA Figs 11 and 14 (pp. 25, 27: one colour per box with a same-colour frame, no connectors, native pixels). Corner insets are out because 40% of our 0.868 in tile is 25 pt, smaller than any reference inset (AReA Fig 15 p25 30 pt, SEGA Fig 11 p25 37 pt). Per-example scores (MFlow's "+7.1dB") are out because one prompt's scores suggest a ranking the paired statistics do not support.

**R7. Hero.** Full text width, between the author block and the abstract, 3.0 to 3.7 in tall, with images on at least 60% of its area. A findings paper pairs the visual evidence with the measured result rather than showing a gallery: at most 20 English words, no printed values on interval marks, "(a) Name" letters, and a caption that walks the panels by letter.
Evidence: SEGA Fig 1, AReA Fig 1, BudgetPix Fig 1 and MFlow Fig 1 (all p1) sit above the abstract (skill rubric: above or directly under it, at least 0.9 width); heights SEGA 0.33 of the page, MFlow 172 pt, LILIE 223 pt; guide rule 5 (images on at least 60%); LILIE Fig 1 (results beside samples, one caption sentence per panel) and Looped-DiT Fig 1 p1.

**R8. Method diagram.** Vector (TikZ), read left to right, at most two panels. Blocks are rounded rectangles: corner radius 2 pt, height 14 to 18 pt, border 0.8 pt in the role hue, fill a 20% tint of that hue, on an 8 pt layout grid. Arrows 0.6 pt, straight or with one elbow, small filled heads, at most two styles (solid for data, dashed for a secondary path). Every quantity carries its symbol from the paper's equations; words appear only as 1 to 3 word tags. A real image or measured data flows through the diagram; schematic axes carry no tick labels; at most one zoom level, drawn as dashed lines or a pale wedge. The caption ends by pointing to the appendix material that expands the diagram.
Evidence: skill recipe (8 pt grid, 14 to 18 pt blocks, 0.8 to 1 pt borders, 15 to 25% tints, 0.6 pt arrows, data in the flow, appendix pointer); LILIE Figs 3 and 4 (pp. 2, 4); guide rules 9, 11 and 14; LiFT Fig 2 p5 and UL Fig 1 p3 (math labels, few words); AReA Fig 4 p4 and BudgetPix Fig 5 p6 (left-to-right pipelines).

**R9. Captions.** Below the figure: "Figure N:", a bold lead of 3 to 6 words naming the subject, then the finding as measured in at most 20 words, then the panels in order (by letter or italic position word), what each colour and mark means (crop colours by name), and the disclosures (rows, n, seed, interval type, selection). No quality adjectives; numbers carry claims; no sentence repeats a label still in the figure. Length in whitespace tokens including the label: hero at most 95, other main-text figures at most 110, appendix at most 140.
Evidence: skill (a bold lead on every caption; LILIE Fig 1's "Generation quality with LILIE." walks five panels in 70 words; method captions are a lead plus 2 to 3 sentences); guide rule 12; SEGA, AReA and BudgetPix bold titles; SEGA Fig 2 p3 (box colours named). The references run 25 to 70 words (guide rule 12; main-text medians SEGA 28, AReA 43, Looped-DiT 40, BudgetPix 43, LiFT 64) and keep seeds and counts in the body. Our caps sit above that range because each figure keeps its sample, seed, interval and selection disclosure, and because the text removed from the artwork moves into the caption.

**R10. Size and layout.** Build every figure at print size: full width 5.50 in, or 2.75 in as a wrapfigure for a single-panel plot with at least 8 text lines beside it. The main text needs at least three wrapped floats. An appendix grid that must fit one page may be built narrower (A.1 at 4.85 in). Main-text figures are 1.6 to 4.4 in tall (at most 0.40 of the page); appendix full-page grids at most 0.75. Gutters: images 0.03 in, image groups 0.10 in, plot panels 0.10 to 0.14 in, panels of different kinds 0.12 in; no outer margin; panel letters outside the artwork. Photos: full frames Lanczos-reduced to JPEG quality 94 with 4:4:4 chroma; crops at native pixels.
Evidence: SEGA (all 14 figures full width; main text 0.15 to 0.33 of the page; Fig 8 p22 at 0.83 width and 0.74 height); AReA Figs 1 and 5 (pp. 1, 7: 0.40 and 0.39 of the page); skill and LILIE (five wrapfigures at 0.37 to 0.5 width; the rubric's three wrapped floats); SEGA Fig 14 p27 (native pixels embedded).
